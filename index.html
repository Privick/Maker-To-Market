    Maker-to-Market · A Living Kashi is an illustrative prototype of the recommendation in “From Footfall to Value: Letting Culture Pay the People Who Keep It Alive” by Dev Khemani (VBYL 2027). Listings, people, prices and records are samples. No real booking or payment is made, no personal data is stored, and this is not a government service.

    How it works

    Three steps, fully transparent

    1. 1
      Discover local hosts.
      Find experiences and crafts offered by makers in Varanasi, through this site or any hotel, travel platform or tour operator connected to the network.
    2. 2
      Book on clear terms.
      See the price, the platform fee, the provider payout, the refund policy and the community guidelines before you confirm.
    3. 3
      Know what you are supporting.
      Each booking shows how the money is split, and craft products link to a provenance record that states what has and has not been verified.

    This is an illustrative prototype. No real bookings or payments take place and no personal information is collected.
